### Stub predictor found (tools/rfa_zrule4.py, 1/3 of the 138 families, R tag 4228 elements with a full Z)
All 54 stubbed elements satisfy: R[113:119] == ff*6, H[61]==0, H[64:68]==00000000, H[68]==0xff, R[146:151]==00*5 (H/R = record bytes incl. the u16 tag). Of the 63 kept (unstubbed) elements only 11 also satisfy it, and those 11 are two recurring identical elements: (R len 1498, Z len 384) x8 and (R len 2770, Z len 408) x3. Rule: stub the Z record when the predictor holds and (R,Z) length is not one of those two pairs; the remaining discriminator between them is unknown (byte scan found only id-like noise). Stub = Revit's constant 138 bytes: take any 138-byte Z of a Revit rfa and substitute the element id (twice). Implement in rvtx/rfa.py, then oracle-test families 0080,0081,0149,0151 (refused, R identical) and the 36 refused set.
